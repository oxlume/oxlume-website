A typical week

What the app does while you work

Sunday evening: the plan

The planning agent builds next week from your menu, bookings, local events, weather and last month's results. You get a push notification with the plan. Change anything with a word.

Monday to Saturday: the videos

Each post is composed from your real photos and clips, checked by the reviewer model and sent to your phone. Approve, ask for an edit, or let a format run on autopilot.

Right time: publishing

Oxlume posts through the official Instagram and Google APIs at the hour your audience is awake. TikTok drafts land in your app, ready to post in one tap.

Month end: the report

A one-screen report in plain words: what went out, who saw it, what to do more of. No dashboards to decode.

Inside the app

Everything a small place needs, nothing it doesn't

Weekly plan you can talk to

"Skip Thursday", "push the new cake", "less text". The plan updates in seconds.

Videos from your own pictures

Motion, text, music and the right prices, cut from the photos you already take. Generative visuals are opt-in and always labelled.

One-tap approval

Approve in the app, in WhatsApp or in Telegram. Edits come back by the next morning.

Publishing to three channels

Instagram Reels and posts, Google Business updates, TikTok drafts. Scheduled at the best hour for your neighbourhood.

Reviewer that catches mistakes

Wrong price, typo, blurry frame, a claim that breaks platform rules. Caught before you see it.

Plain-language report

Monthly, one screen, in your language. What worked, what didn't, what next.

By industry

The AI speaks your trade

Cafes · bakeries

Fresh things sell in the morning

Oxlume knows a croissant video posted at 8:10 outperforms the same one at 14:00. It plans around your opening hours, your lunch menu and the market day two streets away.

  • "Just out of the oven" morning video
  • Lunch of the week with prices
  • Weekend hours story
Salons · barbers

Empty slots are the real problem

The planner reads your booking calendar. A quiet Saturday afternoon becomes a "free slots after 15:00" post on Thursday evening, with a before-and-after from this week.

  • Before and after (with client consent)
  • Free slots this week
  • Stylist or barber of the week
Shops · boutiques

New arrivals, shown the day they arrive

Tell the app "delivery came" and tomorrow's video shows five new items with prices. Catalogue photos become "how it looks at home" clips.

  • "Arrived yesterday" quick layout
  • Product of the week
  • Season and weather hooks
Studios · gyms

Show the room, not the slogan

A 12-second look inside at the right hour fills classes better than any motivational quote. The planner works from your schedule and trainer availability.

  • This week's class schedule
  • Trainer tip in 10 seconds
  • Trial class reminder
