How it works

One weekly loop, run by an AI that knows your business

Connect and drop photos

Link Instagram, add your menu or price list, upload phone photos. Oxlume learns your place in minutes.

The AI plans your week

It reads your calendar, weather, local events and what worked last month, then decides what to show and when.

Videos arrive on your phone

Short vertical videos cut from your own photos and clips, with text, motion and the right prices. Approve or ask for a change.

It publishes and learns

Posts go out at the best time for your area. Every result feeds the next week's plan.

Where the AI is

Not a template engine. A planner, an editor and a critic in one app.

Three models work together on every post. A planning agent decides what matters this week. A composing model builds the video from your real pictures. A reviewer checks prices, spelling, blur and platform rules before you ever see it.

Trust by design

Your photos stay yours. Nothing posts without you.

  • Every video is approved by you before it goes live. Autopilot is opt-in, per format.
  • AI-generated or AI-edited content is labelled, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act.
  • We publish through the official Instagram, TikTok and Google APIs. No passwords, no bots.
  • Your images are never used to train models for other businesses.
