Trust and safety

Rules we wrote before the first line of code

An AI that posts on your behalf needs clearer boundaries than a human assistant. These are ours, in plain language, and they are part of the product, not a policy page.

You approve, we publish

Nothing goes live without your tap. Autopilot exists, but you switch it on per format, and you can switch it off in one tap from any notification.

AI content is labelled

Any video edited or generated by AI carries a visible label and platform metadata, in line with Article 50 of the EU AI Act. Real photos with motion and text are still marked as AI-edited. We do not hide it.

Official APIs only

We connect through Meta, Google and TikTok developer programmes, with the permissions they review. We never ask for your password and never run a bot on your account.

Your data, your model

Your photos and results train the model of your business only. Anonymised performance patterns improve the planner for everyone; your images never appear in anyone else's video.

No fake people, no fake claims

The reviewer blocks invented reviews, invented awards, health claims and prices that don't match your menu. Creative mode cannot generate people.

Stored in the EU, deleted on request

Data lives in EU data centres. Disconnect your accounts and we delete your media within 30 days; export is one button away before that.

Security

Boring on purpose

  • OAuth tokens encrypted at rest, scoped to publishing only, revocable from the app
  • Media stored encrypted, access logged, no employee access without a ticket
  • Model calls run without retention at the provider, under enterprise terms
  • Independent penetration test before public launch; summary published here
  • Report a vulnerability: security@oxlume.com
