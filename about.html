About

Small places deserve a presence that runs itself

Oxlume is an AI company building for the two million cafes, salons, shops and studios in Europe that have a great product and no time to show it. The name joins "ox", the animal that does the steady work, with "lume", light. Steady work that puts your place in the light.

Viktor leads product and company. He started Oxlume after years of making AI-generated video and watching the gap between what the tools could do and what a cafe owner could actually use.

He is based in Ukraine, speaks with every beta member personally and answers trust questions himself.

How we work

Five principles

Real over synthetic

Your photos first. Generated content is a tool, opt-in and labelled, never the default.

The owner has the last word

One tap to approve, one sentence to change. The AI proposes, you dispose.

No invented results

No fake testimonials on this site, no fake reviews in your posts, no promised view counts.

Plain language

Reports, settings, legal texts and this website are written for people who run a shop, not for marketers.

Build in the open

Roadmap, changelog and known limits are public. When something breaks, we say so.
