Billing questions

Is the first month really free?

Yes. Beta members get the full plan for one month, no card required. If you stay, billing starts on day 31; if not, nothing happens.

What counts as a "video"?

A finished 9:16 clip of 6 to 30 seconds, published or delivered as a TikTok draft. Edits and re-renders of the same post do not count.

Can I pause for a holiday?

Yes, any time, from the app. Unused videos do not roll over, but the plan pauses with you.

Do I own the videos?

Fully. Download them, reuse them, keep them after you cancel.
